# Aegis402 Wiki

On-chain financial governance middleware for AI agents on Solana. This wiki is the operator-facing companion to the repository. It is written in English and targeted at integrators, auditors, and hackathon judges.

> **Source.** The canonical source of truth for every page here lives in the repo under `wiki/`. Edits made directly in the GitHub wiki UI will be overwritten by the `wiki.yml` workflow.
